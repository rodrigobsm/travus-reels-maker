# Personal rules (example)

Copy this to `~/.travus-reels/creator.md` and edit it. The skill reads it before every reel. It is for things the config can't express: taste, habits, and extra steps of your own workflow. It stays on your machine.

## Style and preferences

- Don't stop to ask what you can decide; deliver and list the doubts at the end (except the brief, the approvals and the publishing date/time).
- Keyword captions in yellow; fixed title always; official logos of every company mentioned.
- Narration must sound like a friendly chat, never read; send me the full narration before editing.
- I post from my phone: always send the light copy.
- About me (for the opinion block): "<your background in one line>".

## Folders

- Each reel gets a subfolder in `<projects folder>/<Name>`. If I already created the folder with the material, use it.

## Networks

- Connected accounts: Instagram @me, TikTok @me, YouTube "<channel>".
- "My networks" = Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

## Extra steps after scheduling or publishing

1. Append a line to `<path>/content-log.md`: `date | reel | topic | hook | post id (time)`.
2. Tell me the post id, the networks and the time.
